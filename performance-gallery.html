CTAG-FX - JAM Performance

CTAG-FX grew out of my interest in exploring and shaping guitar tones through audio effects. It uses AI to interpret text prompts and generate synthesizer parameter configurations, which are then reinterpreted as controls for a tone-shaping audio FX processor.

This video demonstrates how the resulting audio effects can be used in actual guitar performance.

For more details about the research, please visit the project page below.

Measurement Module #2 – Digital Audio Effects

Teaching Assistant | Yonsei University | Fall 2024, 2025, 2026

As a teaching assistant, I introduced students to the engineering principles behind audio effects through hands-on demonstrations.

I conducted in-person laboratory instruction in Fall 2024 and provided video-based demonstrations in Fall 2025 and 2026.
