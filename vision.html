Research Framework

VISION

Musicanism Designer

A researcher and performer who designs technologies to expand the possibilities of musical expression.

The following research concepts illustrate how I could pursue this vision.

PILLAR 01

Intent-Aware Audio FX

DAI — DI × AI

Research Motivation

AI-driven audio effects research has largely focused on software-based effects and parameter optimization. What if existing physical effects pedals could understand and adapt to a performer's musical intent in real time?

Proposed Approach

Explore an AI-enabled hardware interface that integrates existing digital effects pedals and adaptively controls their parameters based on a performer's musical intent and performance context. The inferred musical intent could also be shared with other wearable and interactive devices.

Relevant Technologies
  • CLAP
  • LLMs
  • Real-Time MIR
  • Performance-Intent Inference
  • Interactive Machine Learning
  • Adaptive Control & Optimization
  • MIDI Device Abstraction
Expected Contributions

Extend AI-driven audio effects control into physical performance environments and enable more expressive and interconnected interactions among performers, audio effects, and other interactive systems.

PILLAR 02

Expressive Wearable Device

PERSON A — A New Face for Musical Expression

Research Motivation

VTubers use virtual avatars to express themselves and interact with audiences through animated characters. What if we could bring the expressive freedom of these characters into the physical world, allowing musicians to become cartoon-like characters during live streams or even on stage while preserving their physical presence and musical expression?

Proposed Approach

Explore an interactive wearable mask that translates facial movements into cartoon-like expressions and dynamically adapts its visual responses to the musical context of a live performance. The design would also consider acoustic transparency, breathability, and ergonomics to support natural musical performance.

Relevant Technologies
  • Wearable Sensing
  • Facial Movement Recognition
  • Multimodal AI
  • Real-Time MIR
  • Embedded Systems
  • Real-Time Visual Rendering
  • Acoustical Engineering
  • Ergonomic Design
Expected Contributions

Explore new forms of embodied musical expression and performer–audience communication by integrating facial expressions, musical context, and interactive visual feedback into a wearable performance interface.

PILLAR 03

Musical Exoskeleton

Beyond Sound and Facial Expression

Research Motivation

What if we could integrate sonic, visual, and physical expression into a large-scale performance ecosystem? Could musicians become living musical characters whose sound, appearance, and movements evolve together?

Proposed Approach

Explore a musical exoskeleton that integrates wearable robotics, interactive visual elements, and adaptive audio effects through a shared musical intent inference system. This system could also interact with other devices across the stage.

Relevant Technologies
  • Wearable Robotics
  • Biomechatronics
  • Human–Robot Interaction
  • Multimodal Sensing
  • Mechanical Design
  • Adaptive Control
  • Embedded Systems
  • Ergonomic Design
  • Interconnected Systems
  • ...
Expected Contributions

Explore new forms of musical performance in which sonic, visual, and physical expression are interconnected, expanding the expressive possibilities of both performers and their surrounding environments.

Future Contributions

Academic Contributions

  • Suggest research directions for AI to better understand complex and abstract human intentions through research on intelligent systems that interpret musical and artistic intent.
  • Pursue a research approach in which scientific inquiry and musical practice advance each other by connecting performance experiences and audience feedback to new research questions and technological improvements.

Artistic Contributions

  • Create new forms of performance and artistic experiences that were previously difficult to realize.
  • Support musicians in creating richer, larger-scale performances by overcoming the physical and technological constraints of artistic expression.
  • Amplify artistic synergy through human–AI interaction by embracing AI as a creative partner rather than a substitute for humans.

Community Contributions

  • Expand creative possibilities and technological accessibility for musicians and artists by sharing research outcomes and developed technologies through open-source initiatives.
  • Contribute to the emergence of new creative fields and career opportunities by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration between musicians and engineers.
  • Expand interdisciplinary educational opportunities for musicians interested in engineering and engineers interested in music.
  • Foster closer and more horizontal relationships between artists and audiences by bridging psychological and social distances through performance, technology, and meaningful communication.
